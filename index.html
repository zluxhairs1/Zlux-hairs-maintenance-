HAIR MAINTENANCE

Guide ♡
KEEP YOUR CROWN LOOKING LUXURIOUS.
01

STRAIGHT HAIR

  • ✓Apply a heat protectant spray, then follow with a small amount of hair serum.
  • ✓Section the hair neatly with a tail comb and straighten each section with a hair straightener.
  • ✓Keep the wig properly stored until your next use.
02

BOUNCY UNIT

  • ✓Brush your wig gently from the tips upward to prevent tangling.
  • ✓Apply your hair serum before or after brushing.
  • ✓Use a heated or heatless curler to recreate the curls, then secure them with pins.
  • ✓Place the unit in a hair net and store properly until your next use.
03

DEEP WAVE

  • ✓Mix your deep conditioner and leave-in conditioner in a bowl of water.
  • ✓Dip the Deep Wave hair into the mixture.
  • ✓Gently detangle from the tips upward using a brush.
  • ✓Style the closure with a hot comb and hair mousse.
  • ✓Define the rest of the hair in sections using your brush and hair mousse.
  • ✓Place the hair in a hair net and allow it to dry completely.
04

PIXIE CURLS

  • ✓Fill a spray bottle with water and a small amount of leave-in conditioner. Lightly mist your curls rather than soaking them completely.
  • ✓Use a detangling brush or octopus brush to brush out and define the curls.
  • ✓Apply mousse and/or argan oil for shine.
  • ✓Style the closure with a wax stick and hot comb.
  • ✓Preserve in a hair net and allow to dry.
